# AMBA-GOV Representative Review Protocol

## Boundary

A representative review must be completed by a real reviewer or documented representative process. The generator must never fabricate a participant, review, consent, demographic characteristic, or outcome.

## Required record

- Review identifier and date
- Reviewer scope or representative role, without unnecessary personal data
- Reviewed workflow and build identifier
- Inclusion questions and observed barriers
- Outcome measures
- Limitations and unresolved issues
- Approval or review status
- Links and SHA-256 values for supporting artifacts

## Privacy

The committed artifact must not contain names, contact information, sensitive personal data, or raw interview notes. Retain any necessary consent or identity record outside the repository under the applicable governance process.
